# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

One person using a browser new-tab page to keep personal, multi-week milestones visible during everyday browsing.

## Product Purpose

Keep a short list of longer-term personal goals present and easy to maintain. Success means goals remain visible and can be added, completed, or removed without leaving the new tab.

## Positioning

A private, local goal list embedded in the browser's most frequently revisited surface—not a full task manager.

## Operating Context

Used briefly whenever a new browser tab opens. Interaction should remain fast and quiet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Simple checklist: add, complete, and delete goals.
- Each goal can contain small repeatable daily steps; completion resets each calendar day.
- Goals and steps persist in browser `localStorage`.
- No account, cloud sync, categories, progress percentages, or due dates.
- Chrome/Brave Manifest V3 new-tab extension built with static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Evidence on Hand

No external assets or factual claims required.

## Product Principles

- Keep long-term intent visible.
- Make maintenance frictionless.
- Preserve privacy through local-only storage.
- Resist task-manager complexity.
